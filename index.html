About

I build and research AI systems: agentic AI, retrieval-augmented generation, LLM post-training and evaluation, and multimodal learning. At Method (Hitachi Digital) I lead end-to-end AI solution development, building AI systems that capture organisational knowledge and turn it into actionable AI capabilities.

Before that I was a Research Scientist at Huawei Noah's Ark Lab, working on LLM reasoning with reinforcement learning and agentic workflows, and a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Queen Mary University of London, working on longitudinal NLP. I hold a PhD in Artificial Intelligence from The Open University (Marie Curie scholarship) on quantum-inspired multimodal representation learning and have published at ACL, EMNLP, AAAI and IJCAI.
